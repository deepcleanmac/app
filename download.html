How to install DeepCleanMac

  1. Open the downloaded fileOpen DeepCleanMac.dmg from your Downloads folder or the downloads list in your browser.
  2. Drag to ApplicationsDrag the DeepCleanMac icon onto the Applications folder in the window that appears.
  3. Launch and scanOpen DeepCleanMac from your Applications folder. If macOS asks to confirm an app downloaded from the internet, click Open. Then start a free scan to see what can be removed.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later · Apple Silicon and Intel
